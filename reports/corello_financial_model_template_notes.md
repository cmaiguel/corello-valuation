## Key Metrics for Series A

Investors will want to see at Series A:
- **ARR** — Recurring software revenue
- **NRR** — Net revenue retention (expansion minus churn)
- **Gross margin** — Ideally >60% blended, >75% software-only
- **CAC payback** — Months to recover customer acquisition cost
- **Burn multiple** — Net burn / net new ARR
- **Runway** — Months of cash remaining
