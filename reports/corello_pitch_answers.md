# Corello — Pitch Language & Investor Answers

**Confidential | Seed Round Preparation**

---

## Primary Pitch

> "We're raising $2.5M at a $12M pre-money valuation. We have $700K in contracted revenue from January to June, plus $300K in NDA/LOI pipeline and $800K in active conversations with manufacturers. This is not ARR; it is commercial validation. The round gives investors 17.24% ownership and funds the conversion of this demand into repeatable revenue."

---

## 30-Second Version

> "Corello is raising $2.5M at $12M pre. We have $700K contracted from January to June — not ARR — plus $1.8M in total commercial visibility. Investors get 17.24% and fund the path to ARR."

---

## Key Investor Questions

---

### "Is the $700K ARR?"

> "That's right to ask. No, we are not calling it ARR. It is contracted revenue from January to June. We also have $300K in NDA/LOI pipeline and $800K in active conversations. We are using this as evidence of demand and pricing the Seed at $12M pre-money, which gives investors 17.24% ownership."

---

### "Why $12M pre?"

> "Because it balances early traction with fair Seed ownership. At $12M pre, a $2.5M investment buys 17.24% of the company. We have $700K contracted from January to June and $1.8M total commercial visibility. We are not pricing this as a mature ARR SaaS business; we are pricing it as an early industrial AI company with strong commercial validation."

---

### "That's a high multiple on $700K."

> "17x on contracted revenue is the sanity check we ran, but it's not the basis of the valuation — and contracted revenue is not ARR. Seed valuations in this category are driven by team, market, and early traction, not revenue multiples. On probability-weighted commercial visibility of $1.08M, we're at 11.1x — and on total visibility of $1.8M, we're at 6.7x. Industrial AI Seed rounds have ranged from $5M to $17M in recent disclosures. Our $12M pre is on the conservative end."

---

### "Can we do this at $10M pre?"

> "We're anchoring at $12M. At $10M pre, your ownership goes to 20% on a $2.5M check — that's at the high end of institutional Seed dilution. We think $12M is fair given the traction. Our internal anchor is actually $14M, so $12M is already a considered, founder-friendly opening."

---

### "What's your gross margin?"

> "We are still building out the full gross margin picture. Our software component has structurally high margin potential — we do not have significant human-in-the-loop delivery costs. AI inference costs are real and we track them. We will have a clean gross margin breakdown before our Series A."

---

### "When will you have ARR?"

> "Our contracted customers are in deployment. As contracts go live and billing begins, the software subscription component converts to ARR. That conversion is the primary milestone this raise funds."

---

## Language Rules

| ✅ Say | ❌ Never Say |
|---|---|
| Contracted revenue | ARR |
| Signed contracted revenue | Run-rate revenue |
| Commercial visibility | Recognized revenue |
| Probability-weighted visibility | Monthly recurring revenue |
| Not ARR | Annual revenue |
