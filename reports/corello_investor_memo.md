Corello is building an AI-native manufacturing intelligence platform for small and mid-sized manufacturers — a sector with significant operational pain, limited technology penetration, and no dominant AI-native player.

## Why Invest Now

1. **Contracted demand is the hardest thing to fake.** Manufacturers have signed. The proof of concept is done.
2. **Manufacturing AI is a large, underserved market.** Incumbents are slow. AI-native approaches are winning early pilots.
3. **Entry at $12M pre is disciplined.** If contracted revenue converts to ARR and grows, the Series A will be at a meaningful step-up. Seed investors enter before that inflection.
4. **17.2% ownership for $2.5M** is a fair Seed outcome — meaningful stake without aggressive dilution.

---

## What This Round Funds

- Convert contracted customer commitments into scalable software deployments
- Build repeatable ARR from existing customer base
- Expand sales motion to 10–15 new manufacturer customers
- Deepen AI product capabilities for manufacturing intelligence use cases

---

## Key Risks (We Are Honest About These)

1. Contracted revenue is not yet ARR — conversion is not proven
2. Some contracts may include services or implementation components
3. Manufacturing sales cycles are long
4. Team is early-stage — execution risk is real

---

## Valuation Basis

The $12M pre-money is supported by:

- **Seed ownership math:** 17.2% ownership is a fair Seed outcome for a $2.5M raise
- **Contracted revenue validation:** $700K in signed commitments demonstrates real demand
- **Market context:** Industrial AI Seed rounds have ranged from $5M to $17M (Allie AI, Manex AI, Matta, Atomic Industries) — $2.5M at $12M pre is on the conservative end
- **Not priced as SaaS ARR:** We are not claiming a 17x ARR multiple. We are claiming early-stage traction pricing.
