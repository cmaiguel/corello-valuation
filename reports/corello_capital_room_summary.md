## Why $12M Pre-Money

1. **Ownership logic** — At $12M pre, investors receive 17.24% on a $2.5M check. That is a meaningful Seed stake, within the standard 15–20% institutional Seed range.

2. **Commercial validation** — $700K contracted Jan–Jun and $1.08M probability-weighted visibility supports a disciplined, early-stage Seed valuation.

3. **Sanity checks (not ARR multiples)**
   - $12M / $700K contracted = 17.1x
   - $12M / $1.08M weighted = 11.1x
   - $12M / $1.8M gross = 6.7x

4. **Benchmark context** — Industrial AI Seed rounds have ranged from $5.2M (Allie AI) to $17M (Atomic Industries). Corello's $2.5M raise at $12M pre is a moderate, early entry in the category.

## Conclusion

Corello should lead with a $2.5M Seed raise at a $12M pre-money valuation. The valuation implies a $14.5M post-money valuation and 17.24% new investor ownership. The support for the valuation is not ARR; it is commercial validation: $700K contracted revenue from January to June, $300K in NDA/LOI pipeline, and $800K in active conversations. The right framing is disciplined: Corello has early contracted demand and a credible industrial AI market position, and the round funds the conversion of this demand into repeatable revenue.
