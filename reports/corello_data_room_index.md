## How to Use This Index

This index tracks the status of every document an investor may request during seed diligence. Update the `Status` column as documents are prepared. Share this index with your legal counsel before your first partner meeting.

**Status Key:**
- ✅ Ready — Polished and investor-ready
- 🟡 Draft — Exists but needs review
- 🔵 Needs Review — Data present but unverified
- ❌ Missing — Does not exist yet
- — N/A — Not applicable at this stage

## What Investors Will Ask First

At an initial meeting, most Seed investors will ask for:

1. **Pitch deck** — Have this polished and ready to send same-day
2. **Product demo** — Live or recorded, under 10 minutes
3. **Contracted revenue evidence** — At least an overview of what the $700K represents
4. **Team background** — LinkedIn profiles + founder bios

At a second meeting / diligence phase, they will ask for:
1. Cap table (fully diluted)
2. Financial model (forecast)
3. Use of funds
4. At least one customer reference call
