| Check Size | Typical Ownership Expectation |

| $1M–$2M (micro-VC / angel lead) | 8–15% |

**Corello at $2.5M / 17.24%** falls squarely in the institutional Seed range. This is neither aggressive nor overly dilutive.

## How to Use These Comps in Investor Conversations

**Do say:**
> "In the industrial AI space, Seed rounds have ranged from $5M to $17M in the last 18 months — companies like Manex AI, Matta, and Atomic Industries. Our $2.5M raise at $12M pre is a more modest, earlier-stage entry — appropriate given we're at contracted revenue, not yet ARR."

**Don't say:**
> "We're valued at Xx ARR like [SaaS comp]" — Corello is not yet generating ARR. Don't use ARR multiples.

> "Our valuation matches [private comp]" — You don't know their actual valuation unless it's publicly disclosed. Stick to round size comparisons.
